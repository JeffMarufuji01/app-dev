# My Favorite Series

## The Quintessential Quintuplets

### Overview
*The Quintessential Quintuplets* is a romantic comedy anime about a student named Fuutarou who becomes a tutor for five sisters.

---

### Why I Like It  
- The story is simple but interesting  
- Each character has a different personality  
- It has a mix of comedy and romance  
- They are quintuplets  
- Fuutarou changes his life after meeting the girl he met five years ago, and he still likes her  
- Yotsuba is the best among the five of them  

---

### Favorite Character
My favorite among the five is **Yotsuba Nakano** because she is my ideal type of girl.  
She is kind, cheerful, and always supports Fuutarou.

> She always puts others before herself, which makes her special.

---

### Ending
1. Fuutarou meets the quintuplets  
2. He helps them improve in school  
3. In the end, Yotsuba is the one who ends up with him  
4. Yotsuba is the one he met five years ago
